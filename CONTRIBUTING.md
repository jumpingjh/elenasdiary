# Contributing to Elena's Diary

Thank you for your interest in contributing to *Elena's Diary*.

Suggestions, bug reports, documentation improvements, and proposed code changes are welcome.

## How to contribute

You may contribute by:

- reporting bugs;
- suggesting improvements;
- improving documentation;
- proposing code changes;
- submitting pull requests.

For significant features or changes, please open an issue or otherwise discuss the proposal before investing substantial work.

## Pull requests

Please keep pull requests focused on a specific change.

A pull request should explain:

- what has been changed;
- why the change is useful;
- whether it affects gameplay or existing behavior;
- whether it introduces third-party dependencies, assets, fonts, audio, or other externally sourced material.

Do not submit third-party code, artwork, music, fonts, sound effects, or other material unless you have the legal right to contribute it and its applicable licensing terms are clearly identified.

## Creative contributions

Please discuss significant narrative or creative contributions before submitting them.

This includes:

- new characters;
- substantial dialogue;
- storylines;
- major narrative changes;
- artwork;
- music;
- major game mechanics tied to the narrative.

This helps preserve the creative and narrative consistency of *Elena's Diary*.
